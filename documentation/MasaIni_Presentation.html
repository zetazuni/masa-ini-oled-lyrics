Lyrics on a 128×64 screen

A lyric video for “Masa Ini, Masa Nanti dan Masa Indah Lainnya” by Nuca. It runs on the OLED already built into the board and is filmed on a phone for an Instagram story.

ESP32-S3SSD1315 OLEDU8g2Python stamper

What sparked it

A song worth sharing

Nuca’s track was the reason to start. I wanted its words to be the star of a story.

A handmade look

A glowing pixel screen instead of a template or an editing app.

A screen already on the board

The Heltec V4 has an OLED built in, so the display cost nothing extra.

A timing puzzle

The board makes no sound, so the lyrics must stay in step with a song playing elsewhere.

Hardware

ESP32-S3R2Dual-core Xtensa LX7 microcontroller. It runs the clock, the state machine and the drawing.
1SSD1315 OLED, 128×64I²C on SDA 17, SCL 18, RST 21. Driven as an SSD1306 through U8g2.
Vext rail, GPIO 36Pulled LOW to switch on the power feeding the OLED.
2PRG / BOOT button, GPIO 0The only control: start, pause, resume, replay.
3USB-CPower, uploading and serial output (USB CDC on boot).
4LoRa radio and antenna portOn the board but unused here. The build still selects the GC1109 PA front end.

Implementation example

Filmed on a phone, in sync with the song

The real run, with the music synced to the display.

  1. Stamp the song. Run the Python tool, press T as the song starts, then SPACE on every line.
  2. Flash the board. Upload with the V4 board, GC1109_PA and USB CDC on boot.
  3. Frame the shot. Put the phone in front of the OLED and cue the song at 1:34.
  4. Press BOOT, play the song. Do both together. The first lyric lands 5 s later.
  5. Record and post. Film the screen with the music, then share it as a story or short.
